Bounce Abyss is an incremental roguelite brick breaker: every run takes you deeper, every upgrade is permanent and there is always something new to unlock.

What's inside

  • BREAKDrag to aim, release and watch hundreds of balls tear through the field. Pick a card every few floors, face guardians every 10 floors and a boss with its own rules at the end of each biome.
  • COLLECT35 balls across 7 elements and 14 fusions: fire that burns, ice that slows, lightning that jumps, poison that stacks. Level them up, earn stars and build your deck.
  • UPGRADEA Workshop for every run, a Forge Tree with 240 permanent nodes, a Laboratory that keeps researching while you are away and a Descent that multiplies everything.
  • AT YOUR OWN PACERuns of a few minutes, playable with one hand and offline. Daily and weekly missions and 80 achievements.
  • FAIRFree to play. No real-money purchases; ads are optional and give in-game rewards. Odds are always on display.
